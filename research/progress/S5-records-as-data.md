# S5: the programme's planning and literature records as checkable data

**Observe.** The roadmap is a scored JSON record built by script with validators; the research crosswalk pins papers
and figures by digest; outside syntheses report figures the programme repeats. Why now: these records are the
programme's memory, and a wrong figure or an unchecked status travels.

**Hypotheses.** H-30 (the roadmap build's validators catch seeded inconsistencies); H-24 (the crosswalk's grounding
method is repeatable on another corpus).

**Data.** A pristine copy of roadmap/ at fce8245b (never the bridge's own); the five papers the owner approved,
held by digest; the three owner-supplied syntheses.

**Experiments.** RL-15 (18 seeded edits in six classes on a scratch copy: shape rules covered, the process rule and the
digest rule not; 0 false positives; two candidate validators proposed); RL-19 (29 figures: 24 found mechanically, 28
of 29 by hand; one figure in all three syntheses is not in its paper).

**Results and proof.** The roadmap build refuses what it can see in one file and not what needs a second record or a
digest check; the syntheses' figures hold except one shared misreading.

**Next.** The two validators if the visionary takes them; a space-aware extractor for a wider figure check.
