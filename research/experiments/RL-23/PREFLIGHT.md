## Finding P1 (charter 1b (v); a protocol defect, caught before any delivery)

The frozen v3 slot form carried no date, so section 5's rule "units after a change are flagged" could not be computed
for slot units: the U3 region of H-40 to H-44 depended on a quantity the pinned form did not hold. Per the charter the
run stopped and the PREREG went to v4, which adds `scopeDate` to `slots[]` and the slot rule to the changes row; no gate
region or threshold changed. Outcome record: attempt 1 (v3) STOPPED at PREFLIGHT, detector LAB_PREFLIGHT, class
CAUGHT_BY_PREFLIGHT; attempt 2 opened on v4. F8 now flags days and slots after a change.

## Evaluator note (an instrument defect, not a protocol one)

The first run read H-37 as U1 on every fixture: the evaluator had applied the fidelity exclusion to pre units, which
carry no intervention by definition, so no pre day qualified. Fixed in the evaluator (fidelity excludes post units
only); the PREREG text was already explicit that exclusion concerns "a unit without its intervention".

## Evaluator note 2 (bridge issue 6, 2026-11-13; an instrument defect, not a protocol one)

The owner's automated reviewer replayed the public fixture through the boundary and the gate and showed that the 2026-11-12
evaluator (9c08695c) read missing evidence as evidence: a missing `fidelity` map or key as exposure, a missing slot
`scopeDate` as "not after any change", and an empty `windows[]` as zero overshoots, so incomplete deliveries could read
SUPPORTED against PREREG section 6 ("a missing field is a missing measure, never a zero") and section 5. The finding is
valid (ACCEPT). The fix keeps the frozen text and makes the gate evidence-aware: `fidelity()`, `day_flag()` and `slot_flag()`
return None when their evidence is absent (a missing account-switch count or `scopeDate` is not computable); H-38's guard
needs a 5-hour window ending on each post day; a piece of evidence absent from every post unit reads UNRESOLVED U2 with the
piece named, and a post unit missing any piece is excluded with the reason and counted in `excluded` (the denominators
`postUnits` and `nPost` are reported), so the remainder faces section 3's minimum (U1). The boundary now refuses a non-ISO
date and `check` prints the COVERAGE counts. Complete-data behaviour is unchanged: F1 to F9 and R1 to R5 are the same bytes
and read the same verdicts. New fixtures: F10 (no fidelity: seven hypotheses U2), F11 (the reviewer's replay, a change plus
no `scopeDate`: H-40 to H-44 U2, H-37 and H-38 U3), F12 (no windows: H-38 U2), F13 (one unit each without fidelity,
`scopeDate` or account-switch count: excluded by reason, H-40 to H-44 U1), F14 (windows on two post days only: five days
excluded, H-38 U1), R6 (a non-ISO `scopeDate` refused). Attempt 2 stays PENDING on v4: no PREREG text changed, no delivery
was opened, and `evaluate` refuses the earlier preflight record by the evaluator digest.
