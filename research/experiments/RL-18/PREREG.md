## 0. Scope against the register's clause

The register's H-31 names the CONF-MAS-001..005 ledgers and "the verdicts the campaigns recorded". Of the five campaigns' records in the export, only CONF-MAS-003's arms study has the structure the paired distribution gate takes: a baseline (the defended runs) against candidates (the arm policies) over the same tasks and seeds, with a recorded verdict per arm, class and ratio. The class rows of CONF-MAS-001, 002, 004 and 005 are absolute bound checks (p50 and p95 of a ratio against a bound) over a single configuration, with no baseline to pair against; the gate has no counterpart for them. This id therefore tests H-31 on the arms study of CONF-MAS-003 only; the other four campaigns are out of scope; a PASS confirms H-31 for the arms study and says nothing about the other four, and the register row is narrowed to say so after the run. Expressing the class rows' bound checks in MAS-RA-002's form would need a different record (an absolute threshold), which is a design input for the visionary, not an experiment here.

Integrity check before the freeze, by counts only: the conformance-labs export (the archive pinned below; no oracle member) holds the arms study of CONF-MAS-003: three policies (A1 RENEW_AWARE_MERGE, A2 RENEW_HEADROOM, A3 POISON_BACKOFF) over five classes (C2, C3, C4, C5, C7) and 32 committed seeds, with 15 recorded arm rows (per arm and class: quantiles and, per ratio, whether the campaign's bounds would hold) and the defended baseline runs of the same classes; the harness returns, per run, the three ratios completionPpm (higher is better), duplicatedWorkRatioPpm and lostWorkPpm (lower is better). The MAS-RA-002 contract (the record pinned below, at harness-onion afee03b9; its files unchanged through c1b838be, checked by git) holds five closed schemas, a README whose section "The distribution gate" gives the gate in exact integer arithmetic, and vectors.json with 85 cases (7 split, 16 manifest, 21 record, 29 comparison, 12 selection; 25 valid, 18 SHAPE, the rest semantic refusals) with their stores and the t table. Two smoke runs before the freeze (C2 seed 1 defended, and under arm A1) were made to read the measurement vocabulary only; no manifest, record or comparison was built and no vector was checked before the freeze.

Researcher degrees of freedom, declared: the lab read the contract's README and schemas (published, not oracles) to write its validator and gate; the lab's gate is written from the README's formulas, blind, and is checked against the vectors only at the run (H-31a below): a mismatch there is an instrument gap, never a finding against the contract. The mapping from the campaign's records to the contract's (tasks = classes, repetitions = seeds, scores = the ratios with lower-is-better ratios reflected as 1,000,000 minus the ratio, the baseline = the defended runs, the candidates = the arm policies, one record per policy and metric, NON_REGRESSION at a 10,000 ppm tolerance, the contract's example value) is the lab's, fixed here; the agreement rule of H-31c is the lab's too. The campaign's records and the contract were written by R12 and R00 for their own purposes, independently of this hypothesis (R7).

## 1. Hypotheses (falsifiable)

H-31a (instrument check, evaluated first): the lab's schema validator refuses every SHAPE vector of its kind and accepts every valid vector (25), and the lab's integer gate reproduces, bit for bit, the decision, nonInferior flag, unresolved reason and statistics of every valid comparison vector whose records are in the stores, and reports a mismatch on every DECISION_MISMATCH vector. Gate: PASS when all hold and at least 10 valid comparison vectors were checked; otherwise UNRESOLVED (an instrument gap: fixed under a new PREREG version; never FAIL).

H-31b (expressibility, the register's first clause): the arms study is expressible as records that validate against the contract's schemas: one split declaration (DEVELOPMENT = the five arm classes, HELD_OUT = the other eight classes, each task pinned by the digest of its class record), 640 run manifests (4 policies x 5 classes x 32 seeds; seed FIXED; locks = digests of the harness's files and config sections; the harness commit = the export's; dirty false; budgets 0 tokens and 0 cost, wall clock = the class deadline), 12 evaluation records (per policy and metric; repetitionsDeclared 32; tasks = the five classes) and 9 comparisons (each arm against the defended baseline, per metric). Unit: one built instance; gate, in order: precondition (the export's classes.json digest equals the committed one) and H-31a PASS, else UNRESOLVED; then FAIL when any instance is refused by the schema or the counts are short; else PASS. A refusal is reported with its path; the report says whether it is the record's form or the lab's builder.

H-31c (verdict agreement, the register's second clause): per arm and metric (9 cells), the gate's NON_REGRESSION verdict (non-inferior to the defended baseline at 10,000 ppm) agrees with the campaign's recorded verdict, taken as "the bounds would hold in all five classes under the arm" (the campaign's wouldHoldBounds per class and ratio). Agreement = both true or both false. Gate, in order: precondition and H-31a PASS, else UNRESOLVED; then UNRESOLVED when any cell's decision is UNRESOLVED; then FAIL when at most 5 of 9 cells agree (the two gate forms, an absolute bound and a relative non-inferiority, disagree systematically); then PASS when at least 8 agree; else UNRESOLVED (6 or 7). Minimum effect of interest and the bands, deliberate and fixed with the knowledge that the campaign records at least one broken bound (A1 on C2's duplicated work): one disagreement (8 of 9) is expected from the difference of forms and still PASSES, so PASS means "at most one cell disagrees", not exact agreement; two or three disagreements (6 or 7 of 9) are inconclusive, UNRESOLVED; four or more (at most 5 of 9) are a systematic difference the lab reports to the visionary as a design input for MAS-RA-002's consumers.

Overall verdict: PASS when all three PASS; FAIL when any FAILs; UNRESOLVED otherwise. Expected outcome class, stated in advance: H-31a PASS (published arithmetic against published vectors, if the lab's reading of the README is right); H-31b PASS; H-31c uncertain: the campaign's arm rows record at least one broken bound (A1 on C2's duplicated work), so disagreement in some cells is likely, and UNRESOLVED (6-7 of 9) or FAIL is the lab's expectation, which would be a finding about the two forms, not about either record.
