H-02b (growth): in the K 6 era (slots 253-278, successful verifies), verify wall time grows with the slot at equal host load by about 13 s per slot, the report's figure. Model: elapsed = a + g x slot + l x load, ordinary least squares on the 23 runs; g's 95 percent percentile interval by a pairs bootstrap over runs (1,000 resamples, seed 20261105; a resample with fewer than 3 distinct slots or a singular system is dropped and counted; fewer than 900 defined resamples is UNRESOLVED). Minimum effect of interest: 5 s per slot either way (at 13 s per slot the floor rule fires every two or three slots; at 8 or less it would fire half as often; at 18 or more twice as often). Gate, evaluated in this order: UNRESOLVED when fewer than 15 runs, or no fit, or fewer than 900 defined resamples; else FAIL when the interval lies entirely outside [8, 18]; else PASS when the interval contains 13, the estimate lies in [8, 18] and the interval is at most 20 s wide (an informative interval); else UNRESOLVED (the interval overlaps the band but PASS is not met). Expected outcome class, stated in advance: with 23 runs, a load coefficient near 50 s per unit load (RL-01) and slot and test count collinear, the interval will be wide; UNRESOLVED is the most likely class.

H-02c (projection and detection delay), descriptive and never gated: for sample fractions 0.05, 0.10 and 0.20 with cadence 10, the kept fraction, argv 63's projected K 8 wall (R00's proportional-scaling method, labelled an estimate), the projected dry-run total (the other argv unchanged; the reader's own seconds excluded, unmeasured), the growth bound per cadence (10 x g), and the detection-delay model for a defect in an attested layer: detected in a verify with probability f, with certainty at the cadence rewrite; P(detected within k verifies) and the expected delay, maximum 10.
